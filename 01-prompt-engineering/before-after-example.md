# Before/After Prompt Improvement Example

- **Before (Weak Prompt):** "Write an email about a delayed refund."
- **Initial Result:** Generic, overly wordy text lacking specific context, timelines, or empathetic professional tone.
- **After (Improved C.A.R.E. Prompt):** "Context: A customer's refund is delayed by 5 days. Action: Write an apology email. Role: Customer Experience Analyst. Expected Output: Formal, empathetic email explaining the delay and providing a new timeline."
- **Improved Result:** Highly structured, empathetic draft containing precise placeholders for dates and a reassuring tone.
- **What Improved:**
  - Added clear role definition ensuring appropriate empathetic tone.
  - Provided explicit context regarding the specific delay.
  - Defined the expected output format for instant usability..
